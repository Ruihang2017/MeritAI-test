- Annual leave, personal/carer's leave, compassionate leave and community service leave follow at least the National Employment Standards (NES). We never reduce an NES entitlement.
- Paid parental leave: 3 months for the birthing parent, 2 weeks for the non-birthing parent / partner, on top of the government Parental Leave Pay scheme.
- Mental wellness day: one paid day per calendar year; it does not carry over; no medical certificate needed.
- Purchased leave: permanent employees can buy up to 2 extra weeks of annual leave per year through salary sacrifice, applied for each May.
- Leave requests go to the Operations Manager at least 2 weeks ahead for planned leave.
- Leave taken under this policy or the NES is never a factor in performance reviews.
