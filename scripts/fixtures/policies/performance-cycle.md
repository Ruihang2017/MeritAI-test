- Reviews are once a year in July, with a short check-in in January.
- Rating scale (5 points): 1 Not meeting expectations, 2 Partly meeting, 3 Meeting (expected for most people), 4 Exceeding, 5 Outstanding.
- Each rating needs at least two specific examples from the period.
