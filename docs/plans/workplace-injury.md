## National model WHS law (Safe Work Australia), checked 2026-09-26

- Notifiable incident: a death, a "serious injury or illness", or a "dangerous incident", even if no one is injured. Report to the regulator immediately and preserve the site until an inspector arrives or directs otherwise (helping the injured person and making the site safe are allowed). https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/duties-tool/construction/duties-information/incident-reporting
- Serious injury includes admission to hospital as an in-patient (any duration; not emergency out-patient treatment), immediate treatment for serious lacerations (deep or extensive cuts that damage muscle, tendon, nerve or blood vessel; not minor cuts), amputation, serious head, eye or burn injuries, degloving or scalping, spinal injury, loss of a bodily function, medical treatment within 48 hours of exposure to a substance. Written notice within 48 hours if the regulator asks. Keep records of notifiable incidents for at least 5 years. https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/sites/default/files/2022-09/Incident-notification-fact-sheet-2015%20UD.PDF
- December 2025 amendments to the model Act (extended absences of 15+ days, suicide, violent incidents or sexual assault, falls, mobile plant) take effect only when adopted into each state's law. https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/safety-topic/managing-health-and-safety/incident-notification
